# Outcome Review Walkthrough

1. Create and activate a plan with a frozen baseline, expected metrics, execution requirement, and multiple review windows.
2. Verify schedules are idempotent and a relative window waits for execution.
3. Confirm execution and advance time through opening, settlement, readiness, and overdue states.
4. Retrieve Investment, Revenue, and Capital measurements; simulate partial and unavailable sources.
5. Add an authorized manual observation and select it explicitly.
6. Verify absolute, relative, and percentage-point variance, lower-is-better direction, tolerance, materiality, mixed summary, and confidence.
7. Complete a review and confirm mutation fails.
8. Create a correction revision, supersede the original, and verify both remain readable.
9. Verify cross-workspace, suspended-member, and operational-admin content restrictions.
