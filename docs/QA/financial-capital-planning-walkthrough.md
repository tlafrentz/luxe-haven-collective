# Financial Capital Planning Walkthrough

- Verify funding sources less reserves, required commitments, and approved allocations reconcile to allocatable capital.
- Confirm pending and restricted sources do not become available and required/discretionary uses remain distinct.
- Exercise request eligibility, expired evidence, candidate readiness, duplicate/funding/reserve/liquidity conflicts, and every alternative including the baseline.
- Verify owner-only approval, expected revisions, idempotent replay, immutable amendments, Decision lineage, Action lineage, releases, and measurement reviews.
- Exercise no plan, no capital, no requests, partial, degraded, permission-limited, conflict, expired, concurrency, loading, and typed errors.
- Confirm sensitive totals, sources, constraints, and allocations do not leak through markup, accessibility output, logs, analytics, or cache scope.
- Validate desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, semantic status, and reduced-motion behavior.
