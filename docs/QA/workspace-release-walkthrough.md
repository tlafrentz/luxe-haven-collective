Validate founding Owner, Administrator, property-scoped Operator, and Viewer journeys. Confirm mutation boundaries, inaccessible-property absence, last-known data under provider degradation, notification eligibility after access removal, and safe preference fallback.

Rehearse fresh and production-shaped migrations; verify grants and RLS for owner, administrator, operator, contributor, viewer, suspended, removed, other-workspace, and anonymous identities. Validate desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion, runtime logs, and temporary-data cleanup.
