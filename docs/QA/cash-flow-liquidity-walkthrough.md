# Cash Flow & Liquidity Walkthrough

Validate `/dashboard/financial/cash-flow` at workspace, authorized-property, single-property, and selected-account scopes.

- Confirm balances come from accounts and opening cash plus statement movement reconciles to closing cash.
- Confirm matched internal transfers do not inflate consolidated flows; unmatched transfers remain visible.
- Verify operating, investing, financing, and other classifications, property attribution, and unallocated shared activity.
- Exercise missing balances, transactions, obligations, reserve policy, stale source, unreconciled, permission-limited, currency, loading, empty, and error states.
- Confirm restricted account and transaction values do not appear in HTML, accessibility output, charts, totals, ranks, logs, or analytics.
- Validate keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion, mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts.
