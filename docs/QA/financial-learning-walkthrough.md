- Confirm expectations and versions do not change when actuals or reviews are appended.
- Validate readiness gates for execution, elapsed time, evidence, confidence, and stale data.
- Check success states, forecast absolute and percentage error, bias, accuracy classification, trend, ROI, payback, and assumption states.

- Exercise empty, partial, degraded, permission-limited, loading, and typed-error states.
- Confirm restricted financial details do not leak through markup, accessibility output, logs, analytics, or cache scope.
- Validate desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, semantic statuses, and reduced motion.
