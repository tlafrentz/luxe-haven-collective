## Findings

### `/reports/shared/[token]`
**[Critical] [Functionality]** The public report-sharing feature is blocked by its own auth middleware — external recipients can't open a shared link at all
- What: Navigating to `/reports/shared/<any-token>` (tested with a fake token) redirects straight to `/login`, even though this page is purpose-built for unauthenticated, token-based access — it lives in the `(public)` route group, has its own "link expired/revoked" handling, confidentiality watermarking, and recipient labeling (`src/app/(public)/reports/shared/[token]/page.tsx`). None of that code ever runs, because the request never gets past the auth middleware.
- Evidence: `src/lib/auth/roles.ts:10` — `protectedRoutes` includes the literal string `"/reports"`, and `isProtectedRoute()` matches any path starting with `/reports/`. That's meant to gate the authenticated dashboard reports area, but it also catches this unrelated public share route because they happen to share the `/reports` prefix. Confirmed by contrast: `/shared/investment-report/[shareId]/[secret]` — the *other* "shared report" feature in this same codebase, not under the `/reports` prefix — works correctly and shows a clean "Shared report unavailable" message for an invalid token instead of redirecting to login.
- Why it matters: this isn't a cosmetic bug — it makes an entire built feature (sharing a report with someone outside the org) completely non-functional for its actual audience. Anyone who receives a shared-report link and isn't already a logged-in Luxe Haven user hits a login wall instead of the report, with no indication why.
- Suggested fix: the prefix match in `isProtectedRoute` is too broad. Either scope the protected prefix to the specific authenticated path (e.g. `/dashboard/reports` if that's what's actually meant to be gated, rather than bare `/reports`), or explicitly carve out `/reports/shared` as a public exception before the prefix check runs.

### `/products`, `/pricing` (alias of `/products`)
**[High] [Content]** Shows a completely different, apparently stale product catalog than `/store`
- What: `/products` and `/pricing` (`src/app/(public)/pricing/page.tsx` is a direct re-export of `src/app/(public)/products/page.tsx`) list two items — "Investment Decision Analysis" ($199) and "Guidebook Design" (custom quote). `/store` (a separate page in the `(marketing)` group) lists five entirely different items — "Modern 2BR Apartment Package," "Revenue Performance Audit," "Investment Decision Playbook," "Custom Digital Guidebook," "HPM Advisory Monthly" — with different names even for what look like the same underlying offerings (e.g. "Investment Decision Analysis" vs. "Investment Decision Playbook," "Guidebook Design" vs. "Custom Digital Guidebook").
- Evidence: `src/app/(public)/products/page.tsx` reads from `getCommerceCatalog(defaultCommerceCatalog)`, where `defaultCommerceCatalog` (`src/platform/commerce/infrastructure/default-catalog.ts:15`) is a hardcoded `InMemoryCommerceCatalogRepository` — static, seed-looking data. `src/app/(marketing)/store/page.tsx` instead queries the real Supabase `commerce_offer_definitions` table live, filtered to `catalog_status = "published"`.
- Why it matters: two public, independently-linked pages (`/products`/`/pricing` vs. `/store`) show a visitor different products, different names, and different prices for what's supposed to be one commerce catalog. Whichever one is stale is actively misleading — either quoting a wrong price or offering something no longer sold.
- Suggested fix: point `/products` (and therefore `/pricing`) at the same live Supabase-backed catalog `/store` uses, and remove or clearly mark `defaultCommerceCatalog` as fixture/seed data if it's meant for tests rather than production traffic.

### `/products`, `/pricing`, `/products/[slug]`
**[Medium] [Functionality]** No site header, navigation, or footer at all
- What: All three pages render as a bare `<main>` with no logo, nav links, sign-in link, or footer — nothing to get back to the rest of the site except the browser back button.
- Evidence: screenshot of `/pricing`; confirmed the same on `/products/investment-decision-analysis`.
- Why it matters: every other public page in the app (marketing, hpm, store, auth) has consistent header/footer chrome; a visitor who lands here directly (a shared pricing link, a search result) has no way to explore the rest of the site.
- Suggested fix: wrap this route group's layout with the same shared header/footer the marketing and store pages use, or confirm this is an intentionally bare embed/iframe-style page (in which case it's not a bug, but that intent isn't evident from the page itself).
