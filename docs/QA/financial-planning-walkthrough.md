# Financial Planning Walkthrough

- Validate workspace, portfolio, selected-property, and property scopes reconcile to canonical actuals.
- Verify approved budgets cannot be overwritten and revisions retain lineage.
- Verify forecast and scenario versions, assumptions, confidence, and evidence.
- Check metric-specific favorable and unfavorable variance, zero baselines, and Planning Health precedence.
- Exercise empty, partial, degraded, permission-limited, currency, loading, and typed-error states.
- Confirm no restricted plan values leak through totals, markup, accessibility output, logs, analytics, or cache scope.
- Validate desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, and reduced-motion behavior.
