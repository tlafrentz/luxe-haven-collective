## Findings

### `/login`, `/register`, `/forgot-password`, `/update-password`
**[Low] [Technical]** Duplicated brand name in the browser tab title (same root cause as the marketing report)
- What: All four pages show a doubled title, e.g. `"Sign In | Luxe Haven Collective | Luxe Haven Collective"`, `"Create Account | Luxe Haven Collective | Luxe Haven Collective"`, `"Reset Password | Luxe Haven Collective | Luxe Haven Collective"`, `"Update Password | Luxe Haven Collective | Luxe Haven Collective"`.
- Evidence: `src/app/(auth)/login/page.tsx:10`, `register/page.tsx:4`, `forgot-password/page.tsx:4`, `update-password/page.tsx:4` all set a `title` that already ends in `" | Luxe Haven Collective"`, and the root layout's title template (`src/app/layout.tsx:12`) appends the same suffix again.
- Why it matters / fix: identical issue and identical fix to the one documented in full in `docs/qa/2026-08-03-marketing.md` (drop the manual suffix from each page's title string) — filed once there rather than repeating the full writeup across every affected area's report. Rated Low here specifically since auth pages are lower-traffic/less SEO-relevant than marketing, but it's the same bug.

## What worked well
- All four pages render cleanly with no console errors.
- `/update-password` renders its form even without a reset-token query param rather than crashing — reasonable defensive behavior (Supabase's client-side auth handles the token exchange).
- The auth boundary itself works correctly: navigating to a protected route while unauthenticated (`/bookings` tested) redirects to `/login?next=%2Fbookings`, correctly preserving the original destination for post-login redirect. This is `src/lib/auth/roles.ts` + `middleware.ts` working as intended.

## Routes skipped
- None of the four auth routes were skipped, but no form was actually submitted: `Create account`, `Send reset link`, and `Update Password` were not clicked, since `.env.local` points at a real Supabase project with no known test accounts — submitting would either fail unpredictably or create a real account/trigger a real password-reset email. Only field presence, labeling, and page rendering were verified.
