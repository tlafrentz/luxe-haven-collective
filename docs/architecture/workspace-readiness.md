# Workspace Readiness

Readiness is a pure policy over capability projections. Precedence is Blocked, Degraded, Setup Required, Attention Needed, then Ready. Stable issue codes are separate from customer copy. Actions are filtered by membership role before presentation.

Persistence stores onboarding review history and issue acknowledgment, recurrence, resolution, and permitted dismissal only. It does not become a competing source for Organization, membership, property, provider, quality, or notification state.
