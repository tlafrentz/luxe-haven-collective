# Learning Measurement

Learning owns the expected-to-observed relationship, not the business metric. An activated measurement-plan revision freezes its baseline, metric definitions, expectations, sources, windows, evidence, and policy version.

The canonical flow is plan activation → execution confirmation → review scheduling → measurement retrieval → measurement selection → metric evaluation → immutable review completion. Measurements remain facts; evaluations remain policy outputs. Provider failure produces unavailable evidence, never a failed business outcome.

Values retain type, unit, currency, qualification, period, source versions, confidence, freshness, and evidence. Corrections append measurement or review revisions and preserve the prior record.
