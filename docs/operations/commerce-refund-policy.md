# Commerce Refund Policy

Refunds are independent, provider-confirmed records. Successful cumulative refunds below captured value produce Partially Refunded; equality produces Refunded. The total may never exceed captured payment.

Failed refunds do not alter a successful Payment. Duplicate updates mutate the same provider Refund record. Refunds do not revoke entitlements or undo fulfillment in PC-001C.3; a later explicit policy owns that effect.
