# Portfolio Outcome Policy

`PORTFOLIO_OUTCOME_POLICY` versions the minimum elapsed time, evidence
requirements, and knowledge maturity thresholds.

A review is ready only when:

- the Portfolio Decision is approved or completed;
- linked execution actions are complete;
- its review/measurement date has elapsed;
- authoritative outcome evidence is available;
- evidence freshness is acceptable.

Outcome success comes from the canonical Decision Outcome classification and
objective assessments. Recommendation calibration is evidence-derived:
successful reviewed outcomes may increase future confidence, missed outcomes
may reduce it, and partial or inconclusive outcomes maintain it pending evidence.
Historical confidence is never edited.

Logs may include workspace, counts, state, policy version, role, duration, and
typed errors. They must omit financial values, sensitive lessons, guest data,
credentials, inaccessible properties, and full evidence.
