# Portfolio Composition Policy

Policy version: `portfolio-composition-concentration-v1`.

- Below 60% evidence coverage: Insufficient Evidence.
- Leading share below 40%: Diversified.
- 40% or greater: Moderately Concentrated.
- 60% or greater: Highly Concentrated.
- 80% or greater: Critical Dependency.
- Seasonal concentration uses the four highest observed revenue months.

Every result includes basis, measured item, leading share, applicable threshold,
evidence references, confidence, and freshness. Thresholds describe dependency;
they do not classify strategic risk.

Archived and excluded properties are absent from current composition because
PI-001A resolves inclusion before this capability. Historical comparisons retain
explicit removed and shifted facts when supplied by the historical projection.
