# Guidebook Publishing Policy

Publishing always requires an authorized human and both publishing and hosting entitlements.

Before publishing:

1. Preview mobile and desktop layouts.
2. Confirm arrival, access, Wi-Fi, emergency, house-rule, and checkout content.
3. Verify every image has meaningful alternative text.
4. Confirm links, maps, contacts, and local recommendations.
5. Remove internal, owner, financial, vendor, and administrative information.

Publishing creates a new immutable version. Corrections require editing the draft and publishing another version. Never mutate a previously published snapshot.

Unpublishing should make the guest URL unavailable without deleting historical versions. Archiving hides the Guidebook from normal Studio lists and does not erase publication history.

Analytics must remain anonymous and operational. Do not store guest names, booking identifiers, precise device fingerprints, or message content.

If maps or media are degraded, preserve the guidebook text and label unavailable content. If publishing fails, leave the prior published version active and keep the new draft recoverable.
