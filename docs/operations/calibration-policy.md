# Calibration Policy

Calibration answers whether confidence or maturity should change. It never changes an algorithm.

Each request preserves the exact lesson revision, prior and proposed confidence, prior and proposed maturity, evidence, reason, reviewer, timestamp, and policy version. Confidence and maturity may advance by at most one configured level per review. Reduction remains possible when contradictory or lower-quality evidence warrants it.

Approval creates a new immutable lesson revision. The original lesson and calibration request remain historically accessible.
