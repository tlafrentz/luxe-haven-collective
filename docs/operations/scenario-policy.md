# Scenario Policy

- A calculated scenario snapshot is immutable.
- Changed financial assumptions require a new Investment Decision Analysis and saved revision.
- The Opportunity current analysis is the preferred scenario.
- Historical scenarios remain viewable and reportable.
- Comparisons require two to four unique scenarios.
- Difference direction is metric-aware: higher revenue, NOI, cash flow, returns, and occupancy are favorable; lower operating expense is favorable.
- Unavailable values remain unavailable and are not treated as zero.
- Evidence limitations remain scenario-specific and reduce confidence.
- Archive is preferred to hard deletion. Permanent removal requires administrative authority and is not exposed in the standard workspace.
