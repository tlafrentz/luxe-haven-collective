# Commerce Access Policy

Active subscriptions grant configured access. Trial access requires an explicit rule. Cancel-at-period-end retains access through the paid period. Past Due receives the configured grace period, initially seven days. Paused and Unpaid suspend access; Cancelled and Expired end it at the policy boundary.

Upgrades add newly configured grants. Downgrades change future access without deleting feature data. Reactivation may resume compatible grants.

Manual grants require an authenticated administrator, subject, reason, scope, duration, and audit event. Test grants never satisfy live access.
