# Product Catalog Policy

- Activated product identity is historical and must not be repurposed.
- Activated prices are immutable; deactivate and create a new version.
- Offers may bundle products without creating false product identities.
- Provider identifiers remain infrastructure references.
- Eligibility, entitlements, and fulfillment are configured through canonical policies and templates.
- Orders may be Draft, Pending Payment, or Cancelled in PC-001C.1.
- Order-line product and price snapshots are append-only.
- Payment, checkout, subscription, invoice, refund, and webhook behavior is intentionally absent.
