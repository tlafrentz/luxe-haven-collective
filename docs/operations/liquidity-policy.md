# Liquidity Policy

Liquidity condition precedence is Insufficient Evidence, Critical, Tight, Strong, then Adequate. It considers available cash, known obligations, configured reserves, operating cash flow, runway, freshness, and reconciliation. Unavailable evidence is never assumed healthy.

Available cash requires complete restriction and commitment evidence. Reserve targets are organization-configured. Burn identifies its measure and lookback; positive cash generation is not burn. Runway requires available cash, negative net burn, sufficient history, and sufficient obligation coverage. It discloses assumptions and exclusions and is not a legal, credit, insolvency, or accounting opinion.
