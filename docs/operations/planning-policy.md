# Planning Policy

Budgets represent intention, forecasts represent expectation, and actuals represent measured history. Approved budgets can only be changed by creating a new draft revision linked to the approved version. Forecast changes create new versions.

Revenue, NOI, cash, and reserve above plan are generally favorable. Expense and capital spending above plan are generally unfavorable. Neutrality and Planning Health thresholds are centralized and versioned. Zero baselines do not produce percentage variance.

Every forecast identifies assumptions, status, evidence, confidence, and scenario. Invalidated or missing assumptions reduce confidence rather than being silently retained.
