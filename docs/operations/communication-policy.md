# Guest Communication Policy

Operators must review all messages before delivery. AI text is a suggestion and may never auto-send in v1.

Only facts in the authorized Conversation, Reservation, Property, and published Guidebook projection may be used. Missing check-in, access, Wi-Fi, or Guidebook facts must remain missing; staff should not infer them.

Internal notes are private, append-only operational records. Never copy them into a guest response without deliberate review. Attachments must be images or PDFs within the configured size limit and remain in private storage.

A draft may be queued only after a scheduled time is supplied. Provider delivery determines Sent and later states. A redirect, local draft, or browser request is never evidence of delivery.

Maintenance, cleaning, owner follow-up, complaints, and lost-property issues should create linked Action Center work while the Conversation continues independently.

When provider synchronization is degraded, preserve the local timeline, identify freshness honestly, avoid duplicate sends, and reconcile by provider message identity before retrying.
