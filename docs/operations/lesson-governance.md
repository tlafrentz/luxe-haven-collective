# Lesson Governance

- Publish only evidence-backed candidates with explicit applicability.
- Treat one review as emerging evidence, not validated universal truth.
- Use versioned policy for confidence and maturity.
- Surface overlapping opposing conclusions as possible contradictions.
- Preserve both lessons until an authorized resolution records refinement, merge, supersession, confirmed contradiction, or retirement.
- Never edit published versions in place or detach historical evidence.
- Require a reason and actor for retirement, merging, applicability refinement, or correction.
- Keep cross-workspace knowledge isolated.
- Operational administration may inspect metadata without automatically exposing customer evidence contents.
