# Capital Allocation Policy

Required uses precede discretionary uses. Candidate readiness considers completeness, evidence, liquidity, reserves, forecast compatibility, source eligibility, dependencies, conflicts, authorization, and freshness.

Every comparison includes Maintain Current Capital Position, Required Only, Base, Growth, Resilience, and Liquidity Preservation alternatives. Recommendations expose requiredness, urgency, impact, risk reduction, alignment, confidence, readiness, liquidity and reserve effects, time to impact, and reversibility. They are planning support, not approval.

Blocking conflicts prevent approval. Partial, phased, and conditional funding must state conditions and cannot assume proportional benefit.
