# Cash Classification Policy

Cash movements are classified as operating, investing, financing, or other adjustments. Economic inflows and outflows remain distinct from internal transfers, reclassifications, adjustments, and unknown activity.

Capital expenditure is investing cash flow. Debt principal, loan proceeds, owner contributions, and owner distributions are financing cash flow. These classifications do not alter Income Statement revenue, operating expense, or NOI. Interest follows the canonical organization policy. Unknown and unmatched activity remains visible and reduces confidence.
