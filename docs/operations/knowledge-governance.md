# Knowledge Governance

- Review low-confidence, stale, contradictory, and unsupported contexts as knowledge gaps.
- Keep confidence separate from maturity and result quality.
- Preserve all historical lesson and review revisions.
- Resolve contradictions through governed relationships; never hide or delete evidence.
- Verify customer routes use workspace authorization before reading knowledge.
- Operational administrators may inspect metadata without acquiring unrestricted evidence access.
- Alert on growing overdue-review, insufficient-evidence, contradiction, and stale-knowledge rates.
