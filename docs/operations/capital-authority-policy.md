# Capital Authority Policy

FI-001F v1 restricts final capital-plan approval and amendments to the active Workspace Owner. Other roles may view, request, create, or review according to explicit permissions.

Approval requires the reviewed revision, selected alternative, available compatible sources, funded required commitments, satisfied hard constraints, current evidence, rationale, outcomes, assumptions, dependencies, conditions, and review date. Commands require an expected revision and idempotency key. Conflicts fail rather than overwrite or duplicate decisions, allocations, receipts, or Action Center tasks.

Approved history is immutable. Material changes create draft amendments linked to the approved plan.
