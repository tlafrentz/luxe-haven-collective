# Financial Metric Policy

## Recognition and inclusion

Revenue uses posted canonical revenue accounts for the selected basis and period.
Pass-through tax, refundable deposits, and gross booking value are excluded
unless a canonical account policy explicitly recognizes them.

Operating expenses include cost of revenue and operating-expense accounts.
Capital expenditures, asset movement, debt principal, and owner distributions
are excluded. Rent may be operating expense when normalized upstream for a
rental-arbitrage property; model-specific rules never live in presentation.

NOI and margin require recognized revenue and at least 80% expense evidence.
Margin is NOI divided by reliable non-zero revenue. Negative results remain
uncapped and presentation uses one decimal place.

Cash balance and net cash movement come only from authorized cash-account
sources. Net movement includes genuine financing, contribution, distribution,
capital, and tax movement but eliminates internal transfers. Neither value is
inferred from NOI.

## Comparison and variance

Previous-period and previous-year comparisons require compatible duration,
basis, currency, scope, and evidence. Budget and Forecast options require a
compatible approved plan. Revenue above plan is favorable, expense above plan is
unfavorable, and cash/NOI above plan is favorable.

Every metric carries current value, compatible comparison when available,
absolute and valid percentage movement, qualification, availability, confidence,
freshness, and evidence. Missing data is unavailable—not zero.
