# Investment Analysis Policy

Investment recommendations are descriptive decision support, not autonomous approvals or financial advice.

- Purchase and Rental Arbitrage use separate financial models.
- The score weights are Revenue 25%, Financial 30%, Market 20%, Competitive Position 15%, and Risk Resilience 10%.
- Risk Resilience is the inverse of canonical risk exposure.
- Blocking evidence gaps yield `Insufficient Evidence`; critical risks may yield `High Risk`.
- Market evidence older than 24 hours is marked stale for the decision projection.
- Provider failures, missing evidence, and stale assumptions are disclosed and never converted into zero values.
- Every result retains property-resolution, market-analysis, evidence, recommendation, and workspace-run lineage.
- Presentation and analytics must not expose inaccessible property or financial data.
