# Outcome Review Policy

- Activate only complete plans with an immutable baseline or an explicit baseline-not-required rule.
- Confirm execution before effectiveness evaluation.
- Compare only compatible definitions, units, currencies, scopes, and periods.
- Use unrounded canonical values; round only in presentation.
- Treat percentage and percentage-point variance separately.
- Version tolerance, direction, materiality, summary, and confidence policies.
- Complete insufficient reviews as Unable to Evaluate with evidence and typed reasons.
- Correct completed reviews only by revision and supersession.
