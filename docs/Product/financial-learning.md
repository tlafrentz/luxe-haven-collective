# Financial Outcomes & Learning

FI-001G closes Financial Intelligence at `/dashboard/financial/outcomes`. It evaluates immutable budget, forecast, capital, reserve, liquidity, and profitability expectations against canonical measured results.

Reviews append variance, success, assumption validation, confidence calibration, and evidence. Structured lessons become workspace knowledge and inform future planning without changing historical budgets, forecasts, expected returns, or approved capital plans. Learning advises future work; it does not create plans or autonomous decisions.
