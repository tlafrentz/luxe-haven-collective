# Relevant Learning

Relevant Learning appears beside Investment, Revenue, and Capital decisions as supporting context. Each lesson shows relevance, applicability match, confidence, maturity, supporting reviews, evidence count, and why it was selected.

Learning may inform narrative, evidence, confidence discussion, and operator attention. It does not silently modify underwriting, pricing, financial projections, recommendations, or approval state.

No-learning, partial, conflicting, degraded, and permission-limited conditions leave the owning capability operational.
