# Financial Planning

FI-001E provides the canonical Budgeting & Forecasting experience at `/dashboard/financial/planning`. It keeps actuals, approved budgets, evolving forecasts, variances, assumptions, and scenarios distinct while reconciling workspace, portfolio, and property scopes.

Approved budgets are historical intentions and are never overwritten. Forecasts are versioned expectations with explicit assumptions, evidence, and confidence. Planning Health describes performance against plan without replacing actual financial truth.
