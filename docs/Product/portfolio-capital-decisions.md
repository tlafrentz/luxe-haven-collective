# Portfolio Capital Allocation & Strategic Decisions

PI-001F moves Portfolio Intelligence from investigation into governed choice at
`/dashboard/portfolio/decisions`.

The experience keeps five concepts distinct:

1. PI-001E risk or opportunity
2. capital-allocation candidate and recommendation
3. approved canonical Platform Decision
4. editable Action Center execution plan
5. expected outcome and review plan

Every candidate includes resource requirements, expected—not measured—impact,
effort, horizon, confidence, assumptions, dependencies, tradeoffs, and at least
one baseline alternative. Approval never moves funds or silently changes a
property strategy. Only an active workspace owner can approve capital in v1.

Proposed and approved capital are available in PI-001F. Committed and spent
capital remain explicitly unavailable until bounded financial integrations own
those facts.
