# Learning Workspace

The Learning Workspace presents authorized organizational knowledge at `/dashboard/learning`. It summarizes completed and ready reviews, validated and candidate lessons, maturity, confidence, contradictions, gaps, trends, and health.

Knowledge is organized by capability and applicability rather than storage hierarchy. Lesson pages explain why a lesson is believed and link directly to supporting reviews, evidence, relationships, history, and activity. Review pages preserve expected-versus-observed evaluation and link to resulting lessons.

Empty, insufficient-evidence, contradiction, degraded, and permission-limited states remain explicit. Presentation never evaluates outcomes or changes knowledge.
