# Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base contains immutable, validated lesson versions. Search covers statements, categories, applicability, markets, properties, strategies, and review references. Filters preserve confidence, maturity, lifecycle, and applicability as independent dimensions.

Every result states where it applies. No lesson is represented as universal unless its explicit applicability says so. Contradictory lessons remain available with their evidence and resolution relationship.

Evidence is read-only. Search results and detail pages are restricted to the caller's authorized workspace.
