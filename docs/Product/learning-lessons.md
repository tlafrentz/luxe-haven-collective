# Learning Lessons

Lessons are evidence-backed interpretations of completed Outcome Reviews. They are separate from measurements and cannot mutate the review that produced them.

An assumption is evaluated as confirmed, partially confirmed, invalidated, or unable to evaluate. That result may produce a candidate. Candidates are not organizational truth. Authorized governance may publish a versioned lesson, refine its applicability, merge related lessons, record contradictions, supersede it, or retire it.

Every lesson displays its category, contextual applicability, confidence, maturity, evidence lineage, source reviews, revision, and lifecycle state. Customer-facing browsing begins in PC-001G.4.
