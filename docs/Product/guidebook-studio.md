# Guidebook Studio

## Product mission

Guidebook Studio is the canonical customer workspace for designing, publishing, and continuously improving premium digital guest guidebooks. It is a hospitality experience platform—not a document or page editor.

The guiding question is: **How do I deliver a consistently exceptional guest experience before the guest even walks through the door?**

Every interaction begins with what the guest needs next. Guidebooks are organized around Before Arrival, Arrival, During Stay, Departure, and After Stay rather than page numbers or document sections.

## Primary users

Owners, property managers, hospitality consultants, guest-experience managers, and designers.

## Ownership and boundaries

Guidebook Studio owns the guidebook, guest-journey sections, content entries, guidebook media, branding, publication versions, QR codes, and guest presentation.

It references properties, reservations, Guest Communications, local businesses, guest recommendations, and Learning Intelligence without owning those records. One property has one primary guidebook. Shared sections, templates, inheritance, seasonal editions, and localization are future capabilities.

## Publishing and improvement lifecycle

1. Draft guest experiences.
2. Review the complete guest journey.
3. Publish an explicit version.
4. Share through guest-facing outputs.
5. Measure guest use and friction.
6. Improve using evidence.

AI may eventually rewrite, suggest content, improve tone, translate, recommend local experiences, and identify accessibility improvements. AI never publishes automatically.

## Primary sections

### Overview

**Mission:** Show whether the guidebook is ready to support guests.  
**Guest-centric question:** Is my guidebook ready?  
**Workflow:** Select a property; review completion, missing experiences, publication status, guest views, QR status, and recent changes; open the highest-impact gap.  
**Future roadmap:** Guest feedback, readiness policy, change comparison, and evidence-backed improvement suggestions.

### Guest Journey

**Mission:** Design the experience guests need at each stage of the stay.  
**Guest-centric question:** What does the guest experience next?  
**Workflow:** Move through Before Arrival, Arrival, During Stay, Departure, and After Stay; define content, media, actions, and resources that reduce friction in each stage; review journey continuity.  
**Future roadmap:** Reservation-triggered content, stage analytics, conditional experiences, seasonal journeys, and portfolio inheritance.

### Content

**Mission:** Manage reusable hospitality information in guest context.  
**Guest-centric question:** What information should guests have?  
**Workflow:** Create or update parking, Wi-Fi, rules, thermostat, trash, laundry, coffee, pool, and appliance guidance; place it into relevant journey stages; attach media; review clarity.  
**Future roadmap:** Shared content, templates by property type, localization, accessibility review, AI rewriting, and designer collaboration.

### Recommendations

**Mission:** Curate distinctive local experiences.  
**Guest-centric question:** What should guests enjoy nearby?  
**Workflow:** Curate restaurants, coffee, groceries, nightlife, parks, activities, and transportation; explain why each recommendation fits the guest experience; publish within relevant journey moments.  
**Future roadmap:** AI recommendations, dynamic context, partner offers, marketplace participation, and measured recommendation outcomes.

### Brand

**Mission:** Make guest presentation feel consistent with the hospitality brand.  
**Guest-centric question:** What should this guidebook feel like?  
**Workflow:** Configure logo, colors, typography, photography, and voice; preview the mobile guest experience; review consistency and accessibility.  
**Future roadmap:** White-label themes, advanced design systems, portfolio brand inheritance, and collaborator review.

### Publish

**Mission:** Deliver an approved guidebook version to guests.  
**Guest-centric question:** How do guests access it?  
**Workflow:** Review readiness; preview guest website, QR code, and printable PDF; explicitly publish a version; share the selected output; retain version history.  
**Future roadmap:** Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, offline mode, native experience, voice assistant, scheduled editions, and approval workflows.

## Guest-facing outputs

The responsive guest website is primary. QR code and printable PDF are secondary outputs derived from the same published version. Future outputs must preserve guidebook identity, property, version, and journey structure.

## Guidebook Health

Health summarizes completion percentage, missing guest experiences, publication status, last update, guest views, and QR status. It prioritizes gaps by guest friction rather than merely counting unfinished pages.

## Empty state

When a property has no guidebook, invite the operator to create the property's first guest experience: “Guidebook Studio helps every guest arrive informed, comfortable, and excited.”

## Learning Intelligence integration

Long term, Guidebook Studio should emit structured observations for guidebook views, searches, repeated page opens, abandonment, guest questions, recommendation engagement, and feedback. Learning Intelligence can compare those signals with guest outcomes and surface evidence-backed improvements. Suggested changes return to Guidebook Studio for human review and explicit publication.

This creates a controlled loop:

Guidebook → Guest experience → Questions and feedback → Learning Intelligence → Evidence-backed improvement → Human-reviewed guidebook version.

## Acceptance criteria

- The customer product is presented as a hospitality experience platform, separate from internal Guidebook Projects.
- Overview, Guest Journey, Content, Recommendations, Brand, and Publish are visible primary sections.
- The core structure follows five guest-journey stages rather than page order.
- Guidebook Health communicates completion, missing experiences, publication status, views, updates, and QR status.
- Property, reservation, messages, recommendations, and learning remain referenced domains rather than duplicated ownership.
- Web, QR code, and PDF outputs are represented.
- Publishing is an explicit human action and is unavailable until persistence and version services are connected.
- Empty states invite creation of a guest experience instead of an empty document.
- Analytics, localization, templates, AI, designer collaboration, inheritance, and Learning integration are documented as future capabilities.
