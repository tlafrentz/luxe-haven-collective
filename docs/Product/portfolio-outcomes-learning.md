# Portfolio Outcomes, Learning & Validation

PI-001G closes the HPM loop at `/dashboard/portfolio/outcomes`.

The capability reviews approved Portfolio Decisions only after execution is
complete, the measurement window has elapsed, and sufficient current evidence
exists. It compares the immutable baseline and expected outcomes preserved by
PI-001F with authoritative measurements from the canonical Outcome system.

Reviews classify results as Exceeded Expectations, Met Expectations, Partially
Met, Did Not Meet, or Unable to Evaluate. Financial, operational, guest,
resilience, and strategic metrics remain independent.

Each immutable review records assumption validation, lessons, surprises, future
guidance, evidence, confidence, and calibration direction. Structured learning
then accumulates into workspace knowledge with Emerging, Supported, Established,
and Well Validated maturity.

PI-001G does not generate recommendations or decisions and never rewrites past
expectations, decisions, reviews, or confidence.
