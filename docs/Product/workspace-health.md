# Workspace Health

Workspace Health composes bounded capability projections into `Ready`, `Attention Needed`, `Setup Required`, `Degraded`, or `Blocked`. Setup and operational health remain separate. Every issue describes cause, impact, affected capabilities, and an authorized recovery action. No opaque numerical score is used.

Dimensions are Identity, Organization, Team & Access, Properties, Connected Systems, Operational Data, Notifications, and Personal Preferences. Valid empty states such as a solo owner, no invitations, or no unread notifications remain healthy.
