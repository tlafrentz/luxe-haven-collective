# Cash Flow & Liquidity

FI-001D answers how cash is moving and whether current liquidity is sufficient. `/dashboard/financial/cash-flow` presents measured cash position, a canonical cash-flow statement, property contribution, obligations, reserves, burn, runway, outlook, attention, and evidence.

Cash is not profit. Balance values come from authorized cash accounts and are never inferred from revenue less expenses. Operating, investing, financing, adjustments, and matched internal transfers remain distinct. Missing balances, restrictions, obligations, or policies render conclusions unavailable or qualified—not zero.

The experience supports workspace, authorized-property, property, and selected-account scopes. Restricted account, transaction, obligation, and reserve values are omitted by the application boundary.
