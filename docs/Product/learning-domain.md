# Canonical Learning Domain

PC-001G.1 establishes the persistence and lineage foundation for organizational learning.

Learning does not own Investment Scenarios, Revenue Recommendations, Capital Allocations, financial plans, communications, Guidebooks, Properties, portfolio decisions, or Actions. It stores typed, version-aware references to those feature-owned objects and relates them across time.

The canonical lifecycle is:

`Observation → Decision → Action → Expected Outcome → Measured Outcome → Outcome Review → Lesson`

Learning Subjects currently support Investment Scenarios, Revenue Recommendations, and Capital Allocations. The subject type registry is additive and already accommodates future financial, guest-experience, Guidebook, Property, portfolio, and operational subjects.

Measurement Plans preserve baselines, expectations, measurement windows, and confidence without evaluating results. Reviews, assumption results, evidence, and lessons are separate append-only records. A correction creates a superseding record.

Lessons require both evidence and lineage. They preserve applicability, confidence, maturity, lifecycle status, source subject, and the exact lineage edges used to derive them.

This milestone intentionally provides no customer-facing Learning workspace and performs no outcome evaluation, scheduling, calibration, search, publication, or feature integration.
