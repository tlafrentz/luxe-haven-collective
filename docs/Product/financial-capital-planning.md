# Financial Capital Planning

FI-001F provides governed Capital Planning and Allocation at `/dashboard/financial/capital`. It combines bounded liquidity, reserve, obligation, planning, portfolio, Decision, and Action projections without recalculating their source truth.

Requests, recommendations, approvals, allocations, commitments, and spending are separate lifecycle concepts. Required commitments and protected reserves precede discretionary allocation. Every approved plan records an authorized human decision, selected alternative, funding, rationale, conditions, expected outcomes, review date, and execution lineage. The capability does not transfer money, procure goods, or autonomously approve investments.
