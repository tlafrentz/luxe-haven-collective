# Outcome Reviews

Outcome Reviews document what was expected, whether execution occurred, what was observed, and how the observation compared with the versioned expectation.

Results are evaluated per metric as exceeded, met, partially met, did not meet, or unable to evaluate. A multi-metric review preserves every result and may summarize to mixed. Actual, estimated, derived, provider-reported, and manual measurements remain visibly distinct. Learning does not claim causation or generate lessons in PC-001G.2.
