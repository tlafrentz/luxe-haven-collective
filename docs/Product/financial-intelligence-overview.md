# FI-001B — Financial Intelligence Overview

## Status

Implemented July 25, 2026.

`/dashboard/financial` is the first customer-facing projection of the canonical
FI-001A Financial Read Model. It answers whether supported operations are
profitable, whether sourced cash is increasing or decreasing, what materially
changed, which authorized properties contribute, what needs inspection, and how
reliable the evidence is.

The page leads with an evidence-backed condition—Strong, Stable, Attention
Needed, At Risk, or Insufficient Evidence—then separates profitability from
liquidity. The condition is not an HPM score, credit opinion, accounting
assurance, or recommendation.

Revenue, operating expenses, NOI, margin, cash balance, and net cash movement
each carry availability, qualification, confidence, freshness, and evidence.
Measured, estimated, projected, forecast, mixed, restricted, and unavailable
states stay explicit. Missing expenses never become zero, missing expenses never
produce false NOI, and cash is never inferred from NOI.

The Overview also provides up to five material changes, classified revenue and
expense drivers, property contribution, known obligations, compatible plan
variance, descriptive attention, canonical Decisions and Actions, and visible
evidence quality. Unconnected sources use intentional empty or partial states.

FI-001B is orientation, not bookkeeping. It does not implement an income
statement, transaction register, reconciliation, cash-flow statement, budget or
forecast authoring, reserve policy, capital recommendation, scenario, tax report,
payables/receivables workflow, or automated journal entry.
