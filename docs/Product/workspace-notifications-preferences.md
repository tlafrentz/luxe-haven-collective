# Workspace Notifications & Preferences

Notifications control events, delivery channels, urgency, frequency, digests, property scope, and quiet hours. Preferences control locale, personal timezone, initial route and property context, density, motion, and intelligence detail. Neither model mutates Organization defaults, Property configuration, canonical timestamps, currency, or stored financial values.

Required access, suspension, provider-authorization, disconnection, and critical-security notifications cannot be completely disabled. Personal subscriptions remain limited to the member's current property access. First use applies quiet role-aware defaults and asks the user to confirm them.

The in-app notification center retains unread/read/dismissed/expired state independently of external delivery. Opening a linked destination always passes through normal authentication and product authorization.
