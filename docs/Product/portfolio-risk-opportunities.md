# Portfolio Risk & Opportunities

PI-001E turns authorized portfolio observations into evidence-backed findings at
`/dashboard/portfolio/risk`.

Risks describe conditions that could materially harm performance. Opportunities
describe credible upside worth investigating. They are separate models and neither
is a decision, recommendation, action, or promise of return.

Each finding names its category, affected scope, impact basis, confidence, horizon,
dependencies, evidence, assumptions, and investigation destinations. The experience
keeps partial value visible when data is degraded and suppresses conclusions when
coverage is insufficient.

The route consumes the canonical Portfolio Projection, Property Comparison, and
Composition projections. Concentration findings are inherited from PI-001D.
