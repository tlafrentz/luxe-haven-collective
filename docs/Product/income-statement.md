# FI-001C — Income Statement & Profitability

## Status

Implemented July 25, 2026.

`/dashboard/financial/profitability` is the platform’s canonical explanation of
business profitability. Financial Overview answers “what is the condition?”;
this statement explains why through recognized revenue, cost of revenue,
operating expense, NOI, margins, category drivers, property contribution,
markets, operating models, and compatible trends.

The hierarchy is Workspace or authorized Portfolio → Property → Revenue and
Expense Category → canonical posted Financial Transaction. Every level
reconciles upward. Properties are ranked only by explicit questions such as
Highest NOI, Highest Margin, Largest Revenue, or Largest Decline—never “best” or
“worst.”

Gross Profit and Gross Margin appear only when Cost of Revenue is modeled
separately. NOI and Operating Margin require reliable expense evidence.
Uncategorized operating costs remain visible. Capital expenditures, debt
principal, owner distributions, asset movements, and cash receipts are not
operating expense.

The statement is profitability analysis, not an accounting ledger, tax report,
budget, forecast, transaction editor, recommendation, or cash-flow statement.
Cash and liquidity remain in FI-001D.
