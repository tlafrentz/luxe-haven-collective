# Investment Decision Report

The Investment Decision Report consumes one calculated Saved Scenario. Preferred is the default selection in contextual workflows, but an authorized operator may choose another immutable scenario.

The report preserves the scenario revision, assumptions, financial result, market evidence, risks, score, recommendation, confidence, evidence gaps, and engine versions. Purchase and rental-arbitrage values remain projected and are never recalculated by Reporting.

External sharing is permitted for authorized users through an expiring, revocable report-scoped link.
