# Brand Foundations

## Product character

Luxe Haven application experiences feel premium, calm, credible, intelligent, operational, deliberate, and hospitality-centered.

They do not feel flashy, playful, generic, excessively corporate, overly technical, or visually noisy.

Premium means:

- clear hierarchy;
- consistent rhythm;
- precise language;
- high-quality evidence presentation;
- restrained color and elevation;
- immediate, predictable interaction.

It does not mean low contrast, oversized whitespace, fragile editorial typography in operational work, excessive rounded containers, or decorative motion.

## Experience voice

Voice is direct, human, confident, evidence-based, and action-oriented.

Prefer:

> Revenue is below target because weekday occupancy declined.

Avoid:

> We detected an unfavorable revenue variance event.

Prefer:

> Connect Hospitable to import properties and bookings.

Avoid:

> Integration unavailable.

## Vocabulary

Use canonical product names:

Workspace, Revenue Intelligence, Executive Intelligence, Portfolio Intelligence, Investment Intelligence, Action Center, Learning Intelligence, Properties, Bookings, Guest Communications, Reports, and Guidebook Studio.

Do not alternate between Messages, Inbox, Guest Messaging, and Communications Center unless a term is an explicitly defined subsection.

Destinations use nouns. Actions use outcome verbs. Statuses describe observable state.

## Brand expression by context

- Customer workspace: warm canvas, restrained premium detail, confident information hierarchy.
- Intelligence: neutral surfaces, legible metrics, visible evidence and interpretation.
- Operations Console: denser/darker treatment is allowed, with identical semantic state and accessibility behavior.
- Reports: editorial composition is allowed within shared typography and evidence rules.
- Guest-facing guidebooks: property branding may vary inside a bounded preview/publishing surface; application controls remain Luxe Haven.
