# Content Standards

Luxe Haven interface language is direct, human, confident, evidence-based, and action-oriented.

## Vocabulary

Use canonical product names consistently: Workspace, Revenue Intelligence, Executive Intelligence, Portfolio Intelligence, Investment Intelligence, Action Center, Learning Intelligence, Properties, Bookings, Guest Communications, Reports, and Guidebook Studio.

Destinations use nouns. Actions describe outcomes with verbs: **Generate Report**, **Invite Member**, **Publish Guidebook**, and **Review Connection**. Avoid vague actions such as “Submit” when the outcome is known.

## Status and guidance

Use precise states such as **Published**, **Draft**, **Waiting for Guest**, and **Attention Needed**. A state communicates:

1. What is true
2. The evidence
3. The impact
4. The recommended action

Help text explains why a choice matters. Errors state what failed, what was preserved, and how to recover. Success feedback states what happened, whether more action is required, and where the result is available.

## Empty states

Every empty state explains what belongs here, why it matters, and what to do next. First-use and empty search results are different states.

Prefer: “Connect Hospitable to import your properties and bookings.”

Avoid: “Integration unavailable.”

Prefer: “Revenue is below target because weekday occupancy declined.”

Avoid: “We detected an unfavorable revenue variance event.”

Generated AI content must be labeled, editable where applicable, and distinguishable from measured facts.
