# Motion

## Purposes

Motion may explain expansion/collapse, panel continuity, loading, progress, confirmation, reorder, and overlays entering or leaving.

## Tokens

- Fast: 120ms
- Standard: 180ms
- Slow: 240ms
- Enter, exit, and standard easing curves are typed tokens

## Rules

- Routine interactions feel immediate.
- Motion is subtle, interruptible, and never required for task completion.
- Long entrance choreography and decorative looping are prohibited.
- Loading preserves final geometry.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable or simplify nonessential transition.
- Success motion confirms an outcome but does not replace text.
- Route transitions never delay navigation.
