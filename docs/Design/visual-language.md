# Visual Language

## Premium

Premium means restraint: fewer accents, generous margins, precise alignment, calm typography, and high-quality state communication. It does not mean gradients, gloss, or large shadows.

## Editorial rhythm

Pages use generous first-viewport spacing, compact capability labels, confident page titles, concise purposes, and clear section breaks. Metrics use tabular figures and consistent optical spacing.

## Color

Neutral surfaces carry most content. Teal guides attention. Gold supports the Luxe Haven brand sparingly. Semantic color always has a text label and never carries meaning alone.

## Icons

Use consistent Lucide line icons at 16, 20, or 24px. Icons reinforce a label or action and remain aligned to the first text line. Decorative icons are omitted.

## Charts

Charts use one primary teal series, optional gold comparison, minimal horizontal grids, neutral axes, editorial whitespace, and compact semantic tooltips. Chart color adapts to dark mode through tokens.

## Responsive composition

Layouts reduce columns before compressing content. Cards preserve internal rhythm at every breakpoint. Tables may scroll or transform into labeled cards; labels and reading order remain intact.

## Visual QA questions

- Is there one obvious focal point?
- Do all edges align to the shared grid?
- Is semantic color earning its presence?
- Does the surface remain balanced in light and dark themes?
- Does content wrap without collision at 320px and 200% zoom?
- Are focus, touch targets, and reduced motion preserved?
